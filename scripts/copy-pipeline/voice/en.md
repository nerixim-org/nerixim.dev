# English voice rules

Audience: EU/US startup founders and engineering leads evaluating a senior contractor (vetted-network profiles link here).

## Register

- First person, plain, direct. Written like a senior engineer's own site, not an agency page
- Concrete over impressive: name the stack, the years, the shape of work — skip adjectives
- Understated confidence. Claims are verifiable facts, not aspirations
- Short sentences win. If a sentence can lose a clause, lose it

## Avoid (AI/marketing tells)

- passionate, seamless, leverage, cutting-edge, robust, empower, elevate, unlock, world-class, delve, landscape
- "not just X, but Y" constructions and negative parallelisms
- Symmetric triads ("X, Y, and Z" more than once per string)
- More than one em dash per string
- Abstract value-prop headlines ("engineering for teams working across languages and markets")
- Anything that reads like a LinkedIn banner

## Prefer

- "I'm a full-stack engineer based in Tokyo."
- "Nine years across web, backend, and AWS. Mostly TypeScript and Rails."
- "Lately I build LLM pipelines that hold up in production — evals, observability, the boring parts."
