## 1. Software development

- Has built the production side: eval harnesses (LLM-as-judge), hallucination-rate monitoring, prompt A/B runs, CI agents for code quality
- Has led an 8-person team's adoption of AI coding tools with measurably faster delivery
- Who it is for: companies that want AI working inside their real operations, not a slide deck

## 3. Localization and multilingual products

- Software localization between Japanese, English and Russian
- Adjusting for the market, not only translating strings
- Designing and implementing i18n architecture
- Multilingual UX
- Who it is for: products entering the Japanese market, or Japanese products going to English- or Russian-speaking markets
