# EN exemplar candidates

Researched 2026-07-12. All URLs fetched and verified live; quotes are copied
verbatim from the fetched page (1–2 sentences each, no full-page copies).
Several plausible-sounding candidates were checked and dropped because they
didn't actually hold up: `jessitron.com/about/` (404), `baldurbjarnason.com`
(no first-person bio prose on the current homepage), `verekia.com` (plain
tone but game-dev portfolio, not the consulting/profile genre), `antirez`'s
site (cert mismatch, unreachable via HTTPS as configured), `www.kalzumeus.com/hire-patrick-mckenzie/`
(404 — page has moved/removed).

Target register (from `voice/en.md`): first person, plain, direct; concrete
over impressive; understated confidence; short sentences; no "passionate /
seamless / leverage / empower" AI-marketing vocabulary.

## Candidates

### 1. Julia Evans
- **URL:** https://jvns.ca/about
- **Author:** Julia Evans (@b0rk)
- **Why:** The reference case for plain, factual engineer-site prose — no framing, just declarative facts about what she does and where.
- **Quotes:**
  - "I'm a software developer. I live in Montreal."
  - "Most of my income comes from my programming zines business Wizard Zines."

### 2. Simon Willison
- **URL:** https://simonwillison.net/about/
- **Author:** Simon Willison
- **Why:** Independent developer/consultant bio, told entirely through roles and projects rather than adjectives — "creator of X," "co-creator of Y," no self-assessment language at all.
- **Quotes:**
  - "Simon Willison is the creator of Datasette, an open source tool for exploring and publishing data."
  - "He is a co-creator of the Django Web Framework, and has been blogging about web development and programming since 2002."

### 3. Brandur (Brandur Leach)
- **URL:** https://brandur.org/
- **Author:** Brandur
- **Why:** About as understated as an intro line gets — one flat sentence, no pitch, no framing.
- **Quotes:**
  - "I'm Brandur. This site is where I publish words and photos."

### 4. Mitchell Hashimoto
- **URL:** https://mitchellh.com/
- **Author:** Mitchell Hashimoto
- **Why:** Senior/well-known engineer stating career facts (co-founded HashiCorp, current project) with zero self-promotion — good model for "claims as verifiable facts."
- **Quotes:**
  - "I'm a developer living in Los Angeles, CA. I previously co-founded HashiCorp."
  - "Today I spend most of my time working on Ghostty, a new terminal emulator."

### 5. Dan Luu
- **URL:** https://danluu.com/about/
- **Author:** Dan Luu
- **Why:** Explicitly anti-self-promotional — credits his blog's reach to "I write, others don't," not to unique expertise. Strong anti-marketing-voice reference.
- **Quotes:**
  - "There's nothing here that most of my co-workers don't know."
  - "It's just that they don't blog and I do."

### 6. Jesse Li
- **URL:** https://jse.li/
- **Author:** Jesse Li
- **Why:** Closest genre match to nerixim.dev — a freelance/consulting engineer's one-page pitch, compressed to almost nothing. Good model for how little a plain, confident consulting page actually needs to say.
- **Quotes:**
  - "I can help you build and break your software."

### 7. Patrick McKenzie (patio11)
- **URL:** https://www.kalzumeus.com/about/
- **Author:** Patrick McKenzie
- **Why:** Senior consultant/advisor register — self-aware, dryly funny, but every claim is concrete and verifiable rather than aspirational.
- **Quotes:**
  - "I'm a better engineer than almost all marketers and a better marketer than almost all engineers."
  - "I'm a recovering Japanese salaryman who ran a succession of small software companies."

### 8. Avdi Grimm
- **URL:** https://avdi.codes/
- **Author:** Avdi Grimm
- **Why:** Independent coach/consultant page — states the service offering (coaching, consulting, workshops) plainly, no "unlock your potential" framing.
- **Quotes:**
  - "Head gardener at Graceful.Dev; Author, 'Exceptional Ruby' and 'Confident Ruby'; coach, consultant, and code cleric."

### 9. Troy Hunt
- **URL:** https://www.troyhunt.com/about/
- **Author:** Troy Hunt
- **Why:** States credentials factually and immediately disclaims overstatement ("I don't work for Microsoft, but...") — good model for understated-confidence phrasing.
- **Quotes:**
  - "I'm Troy Hunt, an Australian Microsoft Regional Director and Microsoft Most Valuable Professional for Developer Security."
  - "I don't work for Microsoft, but they're kind enough to recognise my community contributions by way of their award programs."

## Top 5 to actually use

Prioritizing the closest genre match (freelance/consulting engineer, "work
with me" register) plus the strongest anti-AI-voice references:

1. **Jesse Li** (jse.li) — closest genre match: freelance engineer, one-line pitch, near-zero fluff.
2. **Patrick McKenzie** (kalzumeus.com/about) — senior consultant register, understated confidence, concrete self-assessment.
3. **Julia Evans** (jvns.ca/about) — the cleanest reference for plain declarative engineer-site prose.
4. **Avdi Grimm** (avdi.codes) — consultant/coach offering stated plainly, useful for a "services" section.
5. **Dan Luu** (danluu.com/about) — best anti-self-promotion reference; useful negative-space check against inflated claims.
