
The strongest Japanese portfolios avoid "business-speak" (e.g., *Solution*, *Synergy*) and instead use concrete, action-oriented verbs.

# Research Summary

* **The "Nari" (なり) Perspective:** High-quality bios often frame the engineer’s value through their "stance" (向き合い方).
* **Concrete over Abstract:** Instead of "Digital Transformation," they say "Cleaning up messy codebases" (スパゲッティコードの整理) or "Walking alongside the product" (プロダクトに寄り添う).
* **The "0 to 1" Narrative:** Japanese startups value the phrase "0→1 (ゼロイチ)" to describe early-stage development.
* **Personal Connection:** Strong profiles often include a "Mental Model" or "Values" section to lower the barrier for initial contact.
* **Contextual Suffixes:** Frequent use of ～など (and others) and ～を中心とした (centered around) to show breadth without sounding like an exhaustive, rigid list.

# Useful Patterns

### Self-Introduction

* **Pattern:** State the "Core" then the "History."
* **Phrase:** 「都内を中心にフリーランスとして活動しているエンジニアです。」 (I am an engineer active as a freelance primarily in Tokyo.)
* **Phrase:** 「エンジニア歴10年目で、Webフロントからインフラまで幅広く触ってきました。」 (In my 10th year as an engineer, I've touched everything from frontend to infra.)

### Describing Engineering Breadth

* **Pattern:** Using "Overlapping" skills rather than a list.
* **Phrase:** 「特定の技術に固執せず、目的に応じた最適な選定を心がけています。」 (I don't stick to specific tech; I aim for the best selection based on the goal.)
* **Phrase:** 「バックエンドを主軸にしつつ、最近はAIの導入支援も行っています。」 (With backend as my main axis, I've recently been doing AI implementation support.)

### Describing Startup/Client Work

* **Pattern:** Emphasizing "Velocity" and "Business Value."
* **Phrase:** 「0→1フェーズの立ち上げが得意です。」 (Good at launching 0 to 1 phases.)
* **Phrase:** 「エンジニア目線だけでなく、ビジネスサイドの意図を汲み取った開発を…」 (Developing while understanding business intent, not just from an engineer's perspective...)

### Multilingual / International Work

* **Pattern:** Highlighting "Communication over Translation."
* **Phrase:** 「英語圏のエンジニアとの直接のコミュニケーションが可能です。」 (Direct communication with English-speaking engineers is possible.)
* **Phrase:** 「海外チームとのブリッジ役として…」 (As a bridge with overseas teams... *Note: Bridge is common, but keep it grounded in technical work.*)

# Patterns To Avoid

* **Avoid:** **「課題解決に向けたソリューションの提供」** (Providing solutions for problem-solving).
  * *Why:* Too corporate/agency-style. Better: 「困りごとを技術で解決します」.
* **Avoid:** **「～の架け橋になります」** (Becoming a bridge for...).
  * *Why:* Sounds like a poem. Better: 「仕様の翻訳から実装までスムーズに進めます」.
* **Avoid:** **Overuse of 「支援」 (Support).**
  * *Why:* It sounds passive. Better: 「伴走」 (Running alongside) or 「開発をリード」 (Leading development).
* **Avoid:** **Passive verbs like 「～をさせていただいております」.**
  * *Why:* Too polite/weak for a senior engineer. Use 「～を行っています」 or 「～を担当しています」.

* *Rewrite to:* 「海外の最新ドキュメントやトレンドをいち早く実務に取り入れます」 (More concrete).
